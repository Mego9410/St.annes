# Design brief — St. Anne's website

Single source of truth for design direction. For design or styling work, read this (and any screenshots in this folder) and use the **frontend-design** skill.

---

## Aesthetic direction

- **Primary:** Warm / pastoral — gentle, approachable, church as welcoming place.
- **Secondary:** + Warm / welcoming.

---

## Mood / feel

Welcoming, trustworthy, calm.

---

## What I like

- One strong hero image/section.
- Warm colour palette.
- Subtle motion / scroll effects.
- Varied card/section styles (not all identical).

---

## What I don't want

- Four identical cards in a row.
- Purple gradients / default AI colour schemes.
- Inter, Roboto, or generic system fonts.
- Generic SaaS / corporate look.
- Bland / forgettable.
- Cluttered / busy.
- Cold or impersonal.

---

## Typography

Serif headings + sans body — traditional but clear.

---

## Colour feel

Warm and muted; mostly neutral with one strong accent (e.g. teal or amber).

---

## Hero style

Split — image one side, text the other.

---

## Layout density

Balanced — comfortable spacing, not cramped or empty.

---

## Theme

Light only (no dark mode).

---

## Church / community vibe

Modern community — welcoming, inclusive, active; family-friendly — all ages, accessible.

---

## Buttons / CTAs

Mix of filled primary + outline for secondary actions.

---

## Navigation

Sticky header; simple links — no fuss, easy to scan.

---

## Imagery

People and community (faces, gatherings); mix of imagery — varied but cohesive.

---

## Cards / sections

Rounded corners, soft shadows — friendly; varied — some full-width, some cards, not all the same.

---

## Footer

Useful links — main pages, contact, maybe social.

---

## Motion

Subtle — gentle scroll reveals, soft hovers.

---

## Inspiration links

- [Life.Church](https://www.life.church/)
- [Duna](https://duna.com/)
- [Craft.do](https://www.craft.do/)
